# B3–B4 · Rebuild and independently evaluate
The B3 author uses the current approved handoff for one placement. Save the
rebuild script, construction history and self-check including validity,
reproducibility and source references. The B4 checker checks B2 compliance,
fit reachability and simplification suitability against the source STEP and
the generated shape. Report fit and suitability separately.
Use revise for shape or dimensional rework. If the approved policy needs a
change, the parent reopens the relevant B2 proposal or approval.
