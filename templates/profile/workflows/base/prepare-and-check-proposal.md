# B2 · Prepare, check and approve a placement
The proposer saves the combined contact and simplification plan, revision,
reasons and open questions. The independent checker checks that same revision
and records presentable or revise. Presentable can include explicitly identified
questions for the user. The parent shows presentable revisions one at a time,
records the user's combined decision using the existing review UI or its
confirmation interface, then snapshots the decision for the workflow.
For an approval, include a JSON snapshot of the UI's part confirmations.
Pass --proposal-revision for proposal, check and placement approval.
Archive the two review judgments before contact edits. Reopen affected
placement proposals and repeat checks and approvals when shared conditions
change. After every placement has a current approval, show the complete
assembly and record the user's separate final confirmation with the B3 handoff.
