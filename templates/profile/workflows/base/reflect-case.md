# B6 · Reflect on the case
The parent hands off the case and its evidence. Memory-reflection reads the
history, records proposals in improvements.md and returns them to the parent.
The parent records the user's adoption and scope. Memory-reflection applies
and verifies the adopted changes. Use an independent checker when a tool
change affects geometry generation or judgment. The parent reports the final
results. Record immutable snapshots of proposals, adoption and outcomes for
progress tracking while keeping improvements.md as the live case record.
