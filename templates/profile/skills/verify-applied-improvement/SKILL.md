---
name: verify-applied-improvement
description: Verify an adopted change and record what was confirmed.
---

Check that the adopted change is reachable through its normal entry point.
Resolve skill references, check the procedure against a representative case,
or run the affected tool and compare its results with expected behavior.
If a change affects shape generation or a geometry judgment, hand the inputs,
candidate, and criteria to a checker independent of the author. Preserve the
input geometry and record fit and simplification judgments separately.
Record the evidence, outcome, remaining limits, and changed paths in
improvements.md. Mark pending checks explicitly.
