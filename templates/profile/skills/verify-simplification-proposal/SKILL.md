---
name: verify-simplification-proposal
description: Independently check a simplification proposal before human approval.
---

Inspect the proposal and its contact conditions with tools/review_contacts_web.py.
Check that each set cites the current placement and source faces, that contact
candidates and required openings are protected, and that unresolved mappings
and tolerances are visible. Report omissions and conflicts with evidence.
Leave changes and approval to the responsible parties.
