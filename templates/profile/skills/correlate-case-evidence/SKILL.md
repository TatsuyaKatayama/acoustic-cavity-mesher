---
name: correlate-case-evidence
description: Trace case observations to decisions and artifacts.
---

Read case metadata, logs, review records, validation results, and prior
improvements. Follow the sequence from the initial instruction through
corrections, retries, and the final outcome. Record observations with case-relative
artifact references and distinguish observed facts, inferred causes, and missing
evidence. Include successful practices as well as failures. Use only the figures
or measurements needed to support each observation.
