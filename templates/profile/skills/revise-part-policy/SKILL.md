---
name: revise-part-policy
description: Update reusable simplification policy for a part or part family.
---

Locate the matching policy using b2_part_rules.py and follow the repository's
policy scope and revision rules. Preserve the original instruction, semantic
interpretation, applicability to the analysis and precision, unresolved
criteria, and revision history. Use a family scope when authorized for
different part names. Describe features by function and geometric relationships.
Keep case-specific face IDs in the case evidence and identify any required
remapping or tolerance checks for a new geometry. Link only useful figures
or measurement examples and distinguish policy adoption from case approval.
