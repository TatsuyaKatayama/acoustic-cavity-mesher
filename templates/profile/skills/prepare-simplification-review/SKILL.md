---
name: prepare-simplification-review
description: Prepare evidence and questions for a simplification review.
---

Before simplification review, present proposed keep/simplify/omit sets with
per-part face references, reasons and unresolved questions. Reserve undecided
contact faces and openings. Ask for judgments on ambiguous cases; do not ask
the user to classify every face from scratch. Suggestions do not change saved
decisions or count as human approval.

Use tools/review_contacts_web.py to display the proposal colors and inspect
source face references before asking for confirmation.
