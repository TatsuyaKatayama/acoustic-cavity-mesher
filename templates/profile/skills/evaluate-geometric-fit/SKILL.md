---
name: evaluate-geometric-fit
description: Check geometric constraints and assess fit reachability.
---

Read the source geometry, approved constraints and rebuilt candidate in the same
frame. Check geometry validity, retained surfaces, contact references and
measured fit. Distinguish whether constraints are met from whether parameter
adjustment has reached the best available fit. State missing evidence explicitly.

Mark fit OK when its geometric constraints are met. Report optimization evidence
if available. Unevaluated optimization is informational.
